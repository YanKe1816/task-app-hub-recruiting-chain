Resume Contact Extractor Privacy

App Name

Resume Contact Extractor.

Data Collected

Resume Contact Extractor processes only resume text submitted by the user in the current request. The submitted text may contain candidate name, email, phone, city, and other resume content. The app does not collect external data, scrape websites, or fetch additional data.

Tool Inputs

The tool input field is text: raw resume text provided by the user.

Tool Outputs

The tool output fields are status, candidate_name, email, phone, city, missing_fields, source_text, and errors.

Purpose of Processing

The submitted data is used only to extract contact fields from resume text. The app does not evaluate candidates, rank candidates, make hiring decisions, contact candidates, schedule interviews, send messages, or update ATS or external systems.

Recipients and Sharing

The app does not sell user data, does not intentionally share submitted data with third-party recipients, does not call external APIs, does not send extracted fields to downstream systems, and does not contact external parties.

Retention

The app is stateless and does not intentionally store submitted resume text or generated outputs after request processing is complete, aside from transient platform-level handling or logs for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and service operation.

User Controls

Users control what they submit, may redact sensitive details before submitting, may choose not to use the app, and can contact support for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Login and Accounts

The app does not require user accounts, authentication, or login credentials.

Read-Only Operation and No Side Effects

The app is read-only and does not modify records, send messages, make decisions, contact external parties, update systems, or perform operational actions.

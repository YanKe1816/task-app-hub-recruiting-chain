Privacy

Data processed

Interview Feedback Extractor processes only the interview feedback text supplied in the current request.

Storage and access

The app is stateless and does not store feedback text, extracted fields, or user identifiers. It does not use external APIs.

Purpose

Processing is limited to deterministic extraction of stated strengths, risks, and suggested next steps from interview feedback text.

Resume Contact Extractor Terms

App Name

Resume Contact Extractor.

What this app does

It performs deterministic extraction of candidate contact details from user-provided resume text.

When to use it

Use it when a recruiting workflow needs structured candidate contact fields from resume text.

Input

Provide raw resume text as text.

Output

The app returns structured contact fields, missing field indicators, source text, and structured errors.

What this app does not do

The app does not evaluate candidates, rank candidates, provide hiring advice, contact candidates, schedule interviews, send messages, update ATS systems, or make hiring decisions.

Data handling

The app is stateless and read-only. Users are responsible for deciding what resume text to submit.
